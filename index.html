Bernie — APT repository

Bernie prepares and runs your laser engraving and cutting jobs. This repository installs it and keeps it up to date on Debian, Ubuntu, Linux Mint and Raspberry Pi OS (x86_64 and ARM 64-bit).

curl -fsSL https://beber666.github.io/bernie-releases/bernie.gpg | sudo tee /usr/share/keyrings/bernie.gpg > /dev/null
echo "deb [signed-by=/usr/share/keyrings/bernie.gpg] https://beber666.github.io/bernie-releases stable main" | sudo tee /etc/apt/sources.list.d/bernie.list
sudo apt update && sudo apt install bernie

USB lasers: your account must be a member of the dialout group (sudo usermod -aG dialout $USER, then log out and back in).

En français : ces trois commandes installent Bernie et ses mises à jour sur Debian, Ubuntu, Linux Mint et Raspberry Pi OS. Lasers en USB : votre compte doit faire partie du groupe dialout.
